## 1. Overview

The D4Y Dental Pathway Program would be a structured, digital program designed to support aspiring dental students throughout the dental school application process.

The goal is to create a centralized platform where students can access practical guidance, educational workshops, application resources, practice opportunities, and individualized feedback from people with relevant dental school experience.

Rather than offering one general program that every student must complete, the program would be modular, allowing students to access and pay for the specific areas where they need support.

The program would be hosted through a dedicated website or online platform, allowing D4Y to manage registration, payments, student access, assignments, resources, workshops, and feedback in one centralized location.

## 2. Program goals

The Dental Pathway Program aims to:

- Provide students with a clear understanding of the dental school application process.
- Help students develop a strategic and personalized application plan.
- Support students in understanding school-specific prerequisites and requirements.
- Provide structured preparation for CASPer.
- Help students develop strong personal statements and application entries.
- Connect aspiring applicants with current dental students and dental professionals.
- Provide opportunities for individualized feedback and mentorship.
- Create a supportive community where applicants can learn from one another.
- Make reliable dental school application guidance more accessible and organized.

## 3. Program structure

The program would be divided into several independent but interconnected modules.

### Module 1: Applicant Strategy & Prerequisites

This module would help students understand the overall dental school application process and develop an individualized strategy.

Content could include:

- Understanding the dental school application timeline
- Identifying schools that align with the applicant's goals
- Comparing Canadian and/or American dental schools
- Understanding school-specific prerequisites
- Identifying missing or incomplete prerequisites
- Understanding application deadlines
- Planning DAT/CASPer/application timelines
- Common application mistakes
- Building an application checklist

**Workshop component.** A recorded introductory workshop could walk students through the application process from beginning to end.

Students could then complete a personalized application planning assignment/checklist to identify what they need to complete before applying.

## 4. Module 2: CASPer Preparation

The CASPer module would combine educational content with hands-on practice.

**Workshop.** A recorded or live workshop would introduce students to:

- What CASPer is
- What skills CASPer evaluates
- How to approach different question types
- Time management
- Structuring responses
- Ethical and situational reasoning
- Common mistakes
- Strategies for approaching unfamiliar scenarios

Rather than simply teaching students a formula to memorize, the goal would be to help them develop critical thinking, communication, empathy, and decision-making skills.

**Practice component.** This would be one of the major interactive components of the program. Students could receive:

- Mock CASPer scenarios
- Timed practice questions
- Written-response practice
- Video-response practice where applicable
- Peer discussion opportunities
- Feedback on responses
- Sample high-quality approaches

> Sponsorship with CASPer preparation organizations is discussed in the doc as a possibility. It is internal planning; see the last section.

## 5. Module 3: Personal Statement Lab

The Personal Statement Lab would focus on helping applicants communicate their experiences and motivations effectively.

**Workshop component.** Students would first complete a workshop covering:

- Purpose of a personal statement
- What admissions committees are looking for
- Storytelling vs. simply listing accomplishments
- Demonstrating reflection
- Connecting experiences to dentistry
- Creating a strong introduction
- Developing a cohesive narrative
- Common mistakes
- Editing and revision strategies

**Community component.** Students would have the opportunity to discuss ideas with other applicants and receive general peer feedback. This would create a collaborative environment where students can learn from different perspectives and approaches.

**Individual review.** For students who purchase an individualized review, D4Y could develop a network of:

- Current dental students
- Recent dental graduates
- Dentists
- Experienced D4Y members
- Potentially dental admissions professionals or other qualified reviewers

Students could submit their personal statement through the platform and receive individualized feedback.

Rather than relying on a single reviewer, D4Y could develop a reviewer network, making the program more scalable and allowing students to select the type of feedback they want.

## 6. Module 4: Experiences & Application Entries

This module would focus on helping students effectively communicate their extracurricular experiences within their dental school application.

Students often have significant experiences in:

- Research
- Volunteering
- Leadership
- Employment
- Community involvement
- Shadowing
- Awards
- Clubs and organizations

However, having strong experiences is only one part of the application. Students also need to communicate what they did, what they learned, and why those experiences matter within the application's character and space limitations.

**Workshop topics.** The workshop could cover:

- How to select meaningful experiences
- How to prioritize experiences
- How to describe responsibilities effectively
- Showing impact rather than simply listing duties
- Demonstrating transferable skills
- Connecting experiences to personal growth
- Avoiding repetitive descriptions
- Working within character/word limits
- Common mistakes in application entries

**Individual review.** A higher-tier option could allow students to submit selected application entries for feedback. Reviewers could assess whether entries are:

- Clear
- Concise
- Reflective
- Specific
- Impactful
- Consistent with the student's overall application narrative

## 7. Module 5: Final Application Check-In

The final stage would provide students with an opportunity to bring everything together. (The doc's heading also names a survey feedback form.)

Students could book an individual session with a current dental student, dentist, or qualified D4Y mentor to discuss their application.

Potential discussion topics include:

- Overall application strategy
- School selection
- Personal statement
- Experiences
- CASPer
- DAT
- Interview preparation
- Outstanding application concerns
- General questions about the dental school journey

This could be positioned as a **"Final Application Check" rather than a guarantee of admission or an admissions evaluation.**

## 8. Digital platform

A major component of the program would be the development of a centralized online platform. The website would function as a student portal, allowing D4Y to manage the program from one location.

### Student features

**Dashboard**

- Purchased modules
- Upcoming workshops
- Assignments
- Deadlines
- Submitted work
- Feedback
- Resources

**Module tabs**

- Applicant Strategy
- Prerequisites
- CASPer
- Personal Statement
- Experiences & Application Entries
- Final Check-In

**Payment & registration.** Students could purchase individual modules or bundled packages directly through the website. (Not built in the pilot front end; nothing on the site should imply online payment until the API exists.)

**Assignment submission.** Students could submit:

- Personal statements
- Application entries
- Practice CASPer responses
- Application planning documents

**Feedback.** Students could receive feedback directly through the platform.

## 9. Program access and pricing model

Rather than requiring every student to purchase the entire program, D4Y could use a modular pricing structure.

**Option 1: Individual modules.** Students purchase only the resources they need, for example:

- Applicant Strategy
- CASPer
- Personal Statement
- Application Entries
- Final Check-In

**Option 2: Bundled packages**

| Package | Includes |
|---|---|
| Essential | Educational workshops + resources |
| Application | Workshops + application resources + selected feedback |
| Premium | Full program + individualized reviews + mentorship/check-in |

The exact pricing would be determined after research. **The site shows no prices.** Hardship seats come from the portal prototype.

## 10. Mentorship and reviewer network

One of the strongest components of the program could be its connection to individuals who have successfully navigated the dental school application process.

D4Y could create a database of potential reviewers and mentors, including:

- Current dental students
- Recent dental graduates
- Dentists
- D4Y alumni
- Previous D4Y board members
- Other qualified application mentors

This would allow D4Y to provide individualized support without requiring the executive team to personally complete every review.

Over time, D4Y could develop a formal **D4Y Dental Mentor Network** that becomes an ongoing resource for the program.

> Reviewer compensation is internal; see the last section.

## 11. Community aspect

Although the program would primarily be digital, maintaining a community component would be important. Potential features include:

- Applicant discussion groups
- Peer accountability
- Group workshops
- Q&A sessions
- Dental student panels
- Mentor events
- Application-cycle check-ins

The goal would be to make applicants feel like they are going through the process with a community rather than navigating it alone.

## Pilot program (doc: "Phase 5")

Before a full launch, D4Y could run a small pilot cohort. This would allow the team to evaluate:

- Student satisfaction
- Platform usability
- Workshop effectiveness
- Reviewer quality
- Time required per student
- Pricing
- Areas where students need additional support

Feedback from the pilot would then be used to improve the program before the official launch.

## 12. Proposed outcome

The ultimate goal is to create a sustainable D4Y program that can be offered across multiple application cycles.

Students would leave the program with:

- A clear application timeline
- An understanding of their prerequisite requirements
- Improved CASPer preparation
- A stronger personal statement
- More effective application entries
- Access to dental student/dentist perspectives
- Greater confidence navigating the application process
- A community of peers and mentors

For D4Y, the program supports the organization's mission of supporting students pursuing dentistry.

- 1 to 2 dental students/mentors: final check-in and Q&A
